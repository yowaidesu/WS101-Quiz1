Commitment

We commit to create an evangelized academic community working for the youth, particularly the least favored, by upholding their human dignity, respecting their religious freedom, inculcating a sense of nationhood with a global perspective, and living the Marian virtues.

About NDKC

Notre Dame of Kidapawan College is committed to academic excellence, moral formation, and service to the community.

Students are encouraged to grow spiritually, intellectually, and socially while preparing for meaningful careers.

Our Vision

A Marist community of disciples working together for social transformation towards the fulfillment of the Kingdom of God.

Our Mission

To rekindle the FIRE of God’s love through integral evangelization by providing a responsive, quality Christian education among the young.

Why Students Choose NDKC

Students enjoy a welcoming campus atmosphere, experienced faculty members, and a strong emphasis on both academic and personal growth.
